# 📊 Sales & Collections Performance Hub

## 📌 Project Overview

The Sales & Collections Performance Hub is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to provide a consolidated view of sales collections, customer distribution, collection status, payment methods, and geographical performance.

The dashboard brings together key operational metrics and visualisations to help understand how collections are distributed across customers, cities, regions, time periods, and payment channels. Interactive slicers allow users to filter the report by month, quarter, year, region, and mode of collection, making it easier to explore different aspects of business performance.

The primary goal of this project is to turn raw transactional data into an accessible and structured reporting solution that supports performance monitoring, customer analysis, and informed business decision-making.

By combining KPI cards, trend-oriented charts, status analysis, geographical comparisons, and customer rankings, the dashboard demonstrates how Excel can be used as a practical Business Intelligence tool for sales and collections reporting.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Excel dashboard that consolidates sales and collection data into a single reporting interface. It aims to help business users monitor collection values, understand customer and regional contributions, examine collection statuses, and compare the performance of different payment methods.

The dashboard is designed to answer operational business questions, simplify routine reporting, and make it easier to identify areas that may require further investigation. It also demonstrates the application of Excel-based data analysis, KPI reporting, interactive filtering, and data visualization to a practical business scenario.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

**Step 1: Data Preparation**

Reviewed the source data and prepared it for analysis by checking field consistency, data types, missing values, duplicate records, and the availability of the required reporting dimensions.

**Step 2: Data Organisation**

Organised the data around relevant fields such as collection value, customer, lead ID, date, city, region, collection status, and mode of collection, wherever available in the source dataset.

**Step 3: KPI Development**

Established headline metrics for total collection, total customer count, unique customers, and lead IDs to provide a consolidated overview of the reporting data.

**Step 4: Data Analysis**

Analysed collection values by month, collection status, city, region, payment method, and customer ranking to understand how the reported activity was distributed.

**Step 5: Dashboard Development**

Created KPI cards, charts, and interactive slicers in Microsoft Excel to present the analysis in a single dashboard.

**Step 6: Validation and Review**

Reviewed the displayed figures and visualisations against the underlying data to ensure that the dashboard presents consistent results under different filter selections.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The Sales & Collections Performance Hub demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to transform business data into a structured, interactive reporting solution. By bringing together collection value, customer metrics, collection status, payment methods, city-wise analysis, regional distribution, and top-customer rankings, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of key sales and collection activities.

The analysis highlights differences in collection contributions across customers and cities, variation in collection values across reporting periods, and the distribution of recorded activity across collection statuses and payment methods. These views help users identify major contributors, compare business segments, and determine where further investigation may be useful.

The interactive filters make it easier to explore the data by month, quarter, year, region, and collection method, supporting more flexible reporting and business reviews. The dashboard can therefore serve as a practical starting point for monitoring collection performance and identifying opportunities to improve reporting visibility and operational follow-up.
